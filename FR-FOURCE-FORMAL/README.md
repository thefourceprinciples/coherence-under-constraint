# FR–FOURCE–FORMAL

## Constrained Relational Persistence / Convergent Systemhood

Status: active hostile formalization

This branch isolates the strongest surviving formal content of the broader Fource / Coherence Under Constraint research program and subjects it to explicit redundancy, null, provenance, and falsification tests.

## Governing rule

Fource receives no privileged status because it came first. If an established construct explains the phenomenon completely, the ledger records the reduction rather than protecting the name.

## Current research question

Can methodologically nonredundant system-identification procedures distinguish dynamically privileged organization from arbitrary partitions better than matched null models?

## Current working hypothesis

**Convergent Systemhood Hypothesis (CSH-001):** Dynamically organized systems will produce significantly greater agreement among methodologically nonredundant boundary-, prediction-, causality-, viability-, and robustness-based identification methods than matched null systems, and this convergence will preferentially track organizational architecture rather than microscopic constituent identity.

## Current architecture

- **CUC** — methodological program: study organizational persistence under specified constraints.
- **Convergent Systemhood** — operational criterion: test whether multiple sufficiently independent measures recover the same organization.
- **CHH** — boundary profile: determine where organizational recoverability/persistence ceases under perturbation.
- **Fource** — higher-order hypothesis only: investigate whether the repeated emergence of dynamically privileged, multiply recoverable organization reflects a general feature of constrained complex systems.

No current result requires a new physical force, field, particle, substance, aether, dark component, or violation of established conservation laws.

## Current reduction status

Established machinery already covers large parts of the original intuition:

- structural stability
- persistent topology
- computational mechanics / causal states
- bisimulation and behavioral equivalence
- viability theory
- organizational invariance
- Markov blankets

The surviving target is therefore not generic persistence. It is the narrower question of whether multiple independently motivated criteria converge on the same non-arbitrary organizational partitions and persistence trajectories, including under constituent replacement and adversarial nulls.
