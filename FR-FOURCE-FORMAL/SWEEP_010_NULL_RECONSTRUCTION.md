# FR–FOURCE–FORMAL–010
## The Null Reconstruction After Failure
### What would we build if we had never invented CSH-001?

Status: A0 NULL RECONSTRUCTION / PRE-HYPOTHESIS

CSH-001 remains strongly falsified. This sweep deliberately brackets its convergence operator, thresholds, preferred metrics, and rescue possibilities. The question is not how to repair CSH-001. The question is what an investigator confronting the system-boundary problem from scratch would require before proposing any new hypothesis.

## 1. Start from the phenomenon, not the Fource vocabulary

We observe that physical and dynamical descriptions often admit multiple candidate decompositions. Some decompositions are useful because they support prediction, intervention, compression, persistence, control, or explanation. Different decompositions can be useful for different tasks and scales.

The null reconstruction therefore begins without assuming:

- one privileged universal system boundary;
- one scalar called coherence;
- one latent variable measured by all organizational metrics;
- same-partition agreement across methods;
- constituent independence as a universal identity criterion;
- relational structure as a new physical substance;
- Fource, CUC, aether, dark-sector physics, or any other ontological substrate.

The initial problem is operational:

> Under what conditions is a candidate coarse-graining or boundary privileged relative to a specified task, scale, intervention class, and comparison set?

## 2. Replace universal systemhood with indexed systemhood

A candidate partition P should not initially be assigned an unqualified systemhood score S(P).

Instead evaluate it conditionally:

`S(P | Q, L, I, T, N)`

where:

- Q = question or task being asked;
- L = observational / dynamical scale;
- I = admissible intervention class;
- T = temporal horizon;
- N = explicit null or comparison ensemble.

This indexing immediately explains why two valid methods can disagree without one being wrong. They may be estimating different conditional properties.

Boundary separation may privilege O|E. Intervention structure may privilege A|B. Predictive compression may privilege another coarse-graining. The disagreement becomes a datum to explain rather than an error to average away.

## 3. Treat organization as a hierarchy or atlas, not a winning partition

The natural null object is not one best boundary but an **organizational atlas**.

For every candidate partition or coarse-graining, record a vector of task-specific properties rather than collapsing them immediately:

`V(P) = [B(P), C(P), I(P), R(P), PERT(P), ...]`

The atlas retains:

- which scale each metric privileges;
- which partitions are nested;
- which partitions are mutually incompatible;
- which signals remain stable across time;
- which interventions destroy which signals;
- which apparent signals also occur in matched nulls.

Only after this structure is visible should dimensional reduction or aggregation be considered.

This is fundamentally different from CSH-001. CSH-001 asked whether methods converge on one partition. The null reconstruction asks whether methods jointly reveal a structured map of organizational levels.

## 4. Ground truth must itself be multiscale

A future synthetic positive control should declare its legitimate organizational levels before any metric is run.

For example:

Level L1:
`A = {0,1,2,3}`
`B = {4,5,6,7}`

Level L2:
`O = A + B`

Level L3:
`E = {8,9,10,11}` and the O|E boundary

But unlike CSH-001, these cannot merely be labels attached to coupling strengths. Each level should have a separately specified functional or causal role and a selective intervention capable of destroying that level while approximately preserving the others.

A valid benchmark should therefore include at least:

- an A/B-destruction intervention;
- an O/E-boundary destruction intervention;
- a global destruction intervention;
- constituent substitution preserving relations;
- label permutation;
- observation degradation;
- matched nulls preserving low-order statistics;
- decoy structures sharing one metric signature but not the others.

## 5. Destruction must be level-specific

The key experimental primitive becomes selective ablation.

If A/B organization is claimed to exist, perturb the relations that define A and B while preserving O/E as much as possible.

If O/E organization is claimed to exist, perturb the O–E relation while preserving internal A/B organization as much as possible.

This gives a causal signature:

`targeted intervention -> targeted loss of the corresponding atlas feature`

rather than the CSH-001 assumption:

`generic relational damage -> universal consensus loss`.

A strong organizational measure should be selectively sensitive, not merely globally fragile.

## 6. Null models become metric-specific and agreement-specific

One null ensemble is not enough.

At minimum, a future design should distinguish:

- generator nulls: remove planted organization while preserving degree / weight / activity statistics;
- geometry nulls: preserve candidate partition-size distribution and test split-balance bias;
- temporal nulls: preserve static coupling while disrupting temporal dependence;
- intervention nulls: preserve observational statistics while destroying causal asymmetry where possible;
- agreement nulls: estimate how much cross-method alignment occurs from shared nuisance structure alone.

The last category is directly motivated by CSH-001's failure but follows independently from a general statistical principle: correlated measurements cannot be counted as independent corroboration without modeling their dependence.

## 7. Metrics must earn commensurability

Do not average or rank-aggregate metrics merely because they sound related to organization.

Before combining two metrics, require a theoretical or empirical reason to expect them to identify the same target under the same conditions.

Possible classifications:

- same-target metrics: expected to agree on one organizational level;
- complementary metrics: expected to characterize different properties of the same level;
- cross-scale metrics: expected to privilege different nested levels;
- nuisance-correlated metrics: share bias but not target information;
- antagonistic metrics: improvement in one can systematically reduce another.

Aggregation is permitted only inside a justified equivalence class. Cross-class structure should remain explicit.

## 8. Simple baselines are not enemies; they define the burden of proof

Every richer formalism must be compared against the simplest statistic with access to the same information.

If coupling alone solves a coupling-planted task, a complicated systemhood measure has no demonstrated value.

A future benchmark therefore needs cases where:

- simple coupling succeeds;
- simple coupling fails but temporal structure succeeds;
- observational statistics are identical but intervention structure differs;
- constituent identity changes while organizational role is preserved;
- multiple nested organizations coexist.

The richer framework earns scientific value only if it predicts or discriminates something those simpler baselines cannot.

## 9. Identity becomes a trajectory through the atlas

The earlier identity kernel survives the null reconstruction more cleanly than CSH-001 did.

Token continuity should not be inferred from one static boundary. Instead consider a history:

`A(t) = organizational atlas through time`

and ask whether a candidate organizational level maintains an admissible lineage under transformation.

A provisional identity statement therefore has the form:

> Relative to task Q, scale L, intervention class I, and temporal horizon T, organizational feature F persists through transformation X while remaining causally/historically continuous.

This is deliberately weaker than saying that one metaphysical entity persists independently of all descriptions.

## 10. What becomes of coherence and CUC?

Under the null reconstruction, coherence is not a substance and not yet a universal scalar.

The defensible use is relational and indexed:

> coherence = degree to which specified constraints, dependencies, or organizational relations remain mutually compatible or persist under a declared class of transformations.

CUC can therefore remain as a methodological phrase — coherence under constraint — provided every use states:

- coherence of what;
- under which constraints;
- across what transformation;
- at what scale;
- for how long;
- relative to which null.

Without those indices, the term is too unconstrained to function scientifically.

## 11. What becomes of Fource?

The null reconstruction does not require Fource as an ontological primitive.

The strongest currently defensible role for Fource is as a research-program label for the investigation of persistence, relational organization, constrained transformation, and multiscale identity.

To promote Fource from programmatic vocabulary to physics would require evidence that:

1. an invariant or law exists that is not reducible to established dynamics or information/causal descriptions;
2. it makes quantitative predictions unavailable from simpler frameworks;
3. those predictions survive preregistered tests and adversarial baselines;
4. the effect generalizes beyond generators constructed to contain it.

No result in CSH-001 satisfies those conditions.

## 12. Candidate pre-hypothesis architecture

Without yet naming CSH-002, the null reconstruction suggests a future experimental architecture:

`generator with preregistered multiscale ground truth`
`-> organizational atlas without aggregation`
`-> metric-specific null calibration`
`-> selective level-specific interventions`
`-> simple baseline comparisons`
`-> only then test cross-method relations`

The object of interest would not initially be convergence.

It would be **selective recoverability**:

> Does a method recover the organizational level it is theoretically supposed to detect, and does that signal selectively disappear when the defining relations of that level are destroyed?

This criterion is local, causal, falsifiable, and does not require all methods to agree.

## 13. Anti-contamination rules before CSH-002

No CSH-002 should be frozen until the following are completed without looking at new outcome data:

1. define the multiscale generator and all legitimate target levels;
2. map each metric to its predicted target level before execution;
3. define selective destruction interventions before execution;
4. define metric-specific and agreement nulls;
5. define simple baselines with equal information access;
6. define success and failure independently for each target/metric pair;
7. reserve untouched seeds or an independently generated holdout family for the evidentiary run;
8. publish the dependency/provenance graph showing which predictions were motivated by CSH-001 diagnostics.

The last requirement prevents post-failure lessons from masquerading as independent prior insight.

## Foundation verdict

Starting from scratch, there is no compelling reason to hypothesize a universal same-partition convergence criterion.

The more defensible research object is a **multiscale organizational atlas indexed by task, scale, intervention class, temporal horizon, and null ensemble**.

System boundaries can then be treated as empirically privileged for particular purposes without assuming that reality must contain one unique decomposition.

The strongest candidate experimental principle emerging from the null reconstruction is:

> **A claimed organizational level should be recoverable by methods theoretically sensitive to that level and should selectively disappear when the relations defining that level are destroyed, while unrelated levels remain comparatively intact.**

That is not yet CSH-002. It is the design constraint from which a genuinely new hypothesis may eventually be derived.
