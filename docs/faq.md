# Coherence Under Constraint: public FAQ

## What is CUC in one sentence?

CUC is a formal research framework for asking whether a declared organization remains viable under specified constraints, perturbations, boundaries, flows, and time horizons.

## What is the root invariant?

> A structure persists when its organization survives the constraints acting upon it.

## Is CUC a universal law?

No. It is currently a research framework and family of testable hypotheses. A universal-law claim would require substantially stronger mathematical and empirical evidence.

## Why not just say “resilience”?

Resilience is one outcome: recovery after disturbance within time and cost bounds. CUC also separates continuous persistence, occupancy, robustness, return, adaptation, transformation, boundary behavior, and coherence generation.

## Why is coherence not the definition of persistence?

Because a coherent system can fail synchronously, and a persistent modular system may not be globally synchronized. Coherence is an observable that may support persistence; it is not persistence itself.

## What persists?

A declared organization: a set of relations, functions, roles, invariants, causal responses, or macroscopic patterns. A valid study must define which changes preserve identity and which count as failure.

## What is a constraint?

A condition that shapes admissible states, transitions, or exchanges. Constraints can restrict, route, protect, stabilize, enable, or destabilize organization.

## What is constraint fitness?

The degree to which a constraint architecture permits organization-preserving or organization-restoring transitions. It is conceptually different from raw constraint magnitude.

## What is throughput?

A declared rate of energy, matter, information, work, attention, or another quantity flowing through or within the system.

## Why throughput adequacy instead of throughput?

Because too little flow can starve a system and too much can overload it. CUC does not assume that persistence improves indefinitely with increasing flow.

## What is Fource?

Fource is a proposed local amplitude-weighted, phase-error-limited interaction functional for models with meaningful amplitude and phase variables. It is not currently claimed as a new fundamental force.

## What would show that Fource adds nothing new?

If it provides no reproducible held-out prediction or intervention value beyond ordinary coupling, amplitude weighting, phase error, topology, and noise modeled separately.

## What is the Darkness Functional?

A derived nonnegative diagnostic of modeled degradation processes such as noise, boundary failure, interface mismatch, latency, or hidden stress. It is not a moral category.

## What is the Orbital Coherence Principle?

A validation protocol for bounded relational return. It requires actual boundedness, return within tolerance, meaningful phase or relational state, perturbation survival, and an adequate dynamical model.

## Does CUC say coherence is good?

No. Harmful organizations can be coherent and persistent. Ethical value, consent, autonomy, justice, truth, safety, and ecological effects require independent evaluation.

## Is entropy the same as incoherence or Darkness?

No. Thermodynamic, Shannon, von Neumann, algorithmic, phase, and organizational quantities have different definitions. Any claimed relationship must be derived in a declared model.

## Are the current simulations evidence?

They are toy computational prototypes and provenance. They demonstrate implementation directions but do not empirically validate CUC.

## What are the main simulation limitations?

The existing prototypes confound throughput with coupling, model constraint partly as alignment, rely heavily on one global order parameter, use illustrative thresholds, and have not completed held-out or cross-model validation.

## What is the next experiment?

A modular oscillator benchmark that independently manipulates coupling, constraint, throughput, topology, noise, delay, dissipation, boundary leakage, interface error, and perturbation while comparing CUC terms with strong ordinary baselines.

## How can CUC be falsified?

Individual hypotheses have explicit falsifiers. More broadly, the framework loses distinctive scientific value if its variables cannot be independently operationalized or do not improve explanation, prediction, or intervention over simpler established models.

## Can CUC be applied to biology, cognition, software, or society?

Possibly, but each application requires a fresh literal mapping and domain evidence. Shared vocabulary is not evidence of a shared causal mechanism.

## Does CUC prove artificial consciousness?

No. Memory continuity, identity variables, self-description, or behavioral persistence do not establish subjective consciousness.

## Why use raw LaTeX blocks?

Raw fenced LaTeX preserves exact equation source through copying, version control, Reddit posts, and document conversion. Rendered equations are derivative views, not the canonical source.

## How should I criticize the project?

Identify circularity, unmeasurable variables, hidden confounds, stronger rival models, failed predictions, dimensional problems, ethical risks, or application overreach. Specific falsifying criticism is welcomed.
