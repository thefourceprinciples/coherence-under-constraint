## v1.0 architecture
GardenCore maintains independent diagnostic layers for:
1. local observability
2. statistical recoverability
3. global identifiability
4. approximation obstruction
5. provenance
6. multilabel failure states

## Default comparison
Componentwise / Pareto partial order.
No universal scalarization is permitted unless a domain-specific utility function and its weights are explicitly justified and provenance-tracked.

## Epistemic pipeline
`EXPAND -> ATTACK -> NULL -> COMPARE -> CLASSIFY -> LEDGER -> ITERATE`

## Required controls
- distinguish direct evidence, simulation, reconstruction, analogy, and theorem-level results
- inherited recurrence is not independent corroboration
- track SAR / recursive inheritance risk
- preserve failed hypotheses and negative results
- do not convert class-relative obstruction into theorem-level impossibility
- do not infer global uniqueness from local Jacobian rank
- do not infer practical recoverability from identifiability alone

- nontrivial local kernel implies local non-identifiability in the linearized inverse problem

- global fiber analysis exposed ambiguity invisible to local Jacobian/Fisher analysis in 006

- multilabel diagnostics preserve simultaneous failure modes

- adding observables necessarily improves noisy reconstruction
- adaptive sensing necessarily beats a strong static design

- a universal scalar coherence score is justified
- GardenCore mathematically proves Fource or CUC

PROVISIONAL:
- GardenCore operationalizes selected CUC/Fource questions about distinction survival under constrained transformations

UNTESTED:
- generalization to real measured field data
- measurable advantage on CMB inference over standard cosmological methods
