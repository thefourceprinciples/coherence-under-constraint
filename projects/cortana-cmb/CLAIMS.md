# CORTANA–CMB Claims Ledger

Status labels distinguish established background, results demonstrated by the repository's toy benches, hypotheses to test, and quarantined ontology.

## Established background / adopted method

These are not claimed as project discoveries.

1. Coherence generation, persistence, and recoverability are distinct questions.
2. Coherence and recoverability depend on the relation, scale, representation, observable, and noise model being studied.
3. Linearized constraint and observation maps can be analyzed with operators, singular spectra, rank, conditioning, and null spaces.
4. State reconstruction is an inverse problem whose identifiability depends on the forward model and available observations.
5. Evidence dependence must be tracked when combining constraints.
6. Hidden states require discriminating response signatures; invisibility alone is not evidence for a specific hidden ontology.

## Demonstrated in repository benches

1. In the discretized CMB–010 toy model, 29 temperature multipoles constrain at most 29 directions among 120 primordial-spectrum bins under the stated numerical rank tolerance.
2. CMB–010 separates exact numerical null directions from weakly transmitted directions through rank and relative singular values.
3. In CMB–011, stacking a distinct toy response channel reduces the discretized numerical kernel, while many newly visible directions remain ill-conditioned.
4. The same rank/SVD/null-space diagnostic pattern is executable in the repository's quantum, latent-state, and structural toy benches.

These are architecture and implementation demonstrations, not evidence of new CMB physics or superiority over established domain methods.

## Hypotheses / program claims to test

1. Target-relevant relations may sometimes be reconstructible without recovery of a complete prior microstate.
2. A shared provenance-and-identifiability workflow may improve auditability across multiple inverse-problem domains.
3. The architecture may generate useful measurement-design or sensor-placement recommendations on external, held-out, or field data.

## Negative / limiting results

- The old universal reciprocal phase-strain Fource scalar is not justified as a universal physical law.
- Relational Transfer Spectrum and Chronovisor Kernel use established SVD/null-space mathematics; novelty, if any, is architectural.
- CUC does not replace domain dynamical equations, calibrated likelihoods, uncertainty analysis, or validation.
- Kernel dimensions and condition numbers reported by the toy benches depend on discretization, scaling/whitening, and the stated numerical rank tolerance.
- CMB data do not establish Element-0, Fourcematter, or novel Gravity-Shadow physics.
