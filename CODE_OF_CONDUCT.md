# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

The CUC project is committed to a rigorous, humane, and inspectable research environment. Participants may challenge definitions, equations, evidence, interpretations, and project decisions without attacking the dignity or identity of other participants.

## Expected conduct

- Critique claims specifically and support criticism with reasoning or evidence.
- Distinguish error, uncertainty, disagreement, and misconduct.
- Welcome falsification, replication failure, and negative results.
- Preserve attribution and provenance.
- Disclose conflicts, limitations, and material use of automated tools.
- Respect consent, privacy, and the right to disengage.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or sexualized conduct.
- Do not use project language to diagnose, rank, or dehumanize people.
- Do not equate disagreement with incoherence or moral failure.

## Research integrity

The following are incompatible with participation:

- fabricating or selectively hiding data;
- presenting illustrative outputs as empirical findings;
- silently changing a falsifier after results are known;
- laundering speculation into canonical status;
- plagiarizing work or obscuring provenance;
- using coherence language to justify coercion or suppress dissent.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project communications, public presentations made on behalf of the project, and project-managed research spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until a dedicated moderation address is published, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate GitHub contact channel. Do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban contributors. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and permit appeal when safe and practical.

## Stewardship principle

> Non-distortion, load fidelity, open futures, and clean handoff.
