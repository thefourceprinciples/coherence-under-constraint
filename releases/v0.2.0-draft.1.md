## What this draft establishes

- organization and viability as the primary objects of persistence;
- explicit system, environment, boundary, constraint, perturbation, and horizon declarations;
- continuous, occupancy, and grace-period persistence definitions;
- separate robustness, resilience, return, adaptation, and transformation concepts;
- local, interface, global, functional, informational, and causal coherence templates;
- Fource as a conditional local coherence-generation functional;
- Darkness as a derived degradation diagnostic;
- the Orbital Coherence Principle as a bounded-return validation gate;
- ten registered hypotheses with falsifiers;
- an evidence ladder and promotion rules;
- a modular reference benchmark design;
- raw fenced LaTeX as the authoritative equation source;
- claim, symbol, contribution, governance, and reproducibility infrastructure.

## What this draft does not establish

This snapshot is not:

- empirical validation of CUC;
- peer review;
- a universal physical law;
- discovery of a new fundamental force;
- proof that all systems are oscillators;
- evidence for consciousness or speculative application branches;
- a claim that coherence or persistence is morally good.

The existing simulations are historical toy prototypes. The reference benchmark is designed but not yet implemented.
