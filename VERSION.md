# Version history

## v0.2.0-draft.1 — Charter reformalization

**Draft date:** 2026-07-18  
**Status:** Permanent public draft proposed for ratification; not a final release  
**Canonical snapshot:** `58fedab3b866fbcaceb12bd62ada618c63dde811`

This draft reformalizes CUC around organizational survival rather than treating coherence as persistence itself.

### Added

- CUC Canon v0.2 Charter and research constitution.
- Explicit system, environment, boundary, organization, viability, constraint, throughput, perturbation, and horizon declarations.
- Continuous, occupancy, and grace-period persistence definitions.
- Separate robustness, resilience, return, adaptation, and transformation concepts.
- Local, interface, global, functional, informational, and causal coherence templates.
- Fource as a regularized local coherence-generation functional.
- Darkness as a derived degradation diagnostic.
- Orbital Coherence Principle as a bounded-return validation gate.
- Ten falsifiable hypotheses and an evidence ladder.
- Reference modular-oscillator benchmark design.
- Domain translation, ethics, governance, registry, and pruning rules.
- Copy-safe raw-LaTeX policy and stable equation identifiers.
- Machine-readable symbol and claim registries.
- Public FAQ and Reddit release draft.

### Changed

- Promoted the invariant “A structure persists when its organization survives the constraints acting upon it.”
- Reclassified `Structure = Coherence × Constraint × Throughput` as a historical mnemonic.
- Replaced raw constraint magnitude with constraint architecture and fitness.
- Replaced monotonic throughput assumptions with throughput adequacy.
- Separated persistence probabilities from reduced propensity scores.
- Reframed existing simulations as toy prototypes rather than validation.

### Known limitations

- The Charter remains a draft pending ratification.
- The reference benchmark has not yet been implemented.
- Existing simulation variables are confounded in several places.
- No CUC hypothesis has yet reached prospective empirical validation.
- Cross-domain applications require independent operational mappings.
